## 0.6.14 — 2026-07-28

Added longitudinal safeguards against prolonged semantic lock-in.

- Reclassified extended work on an incoherent coined abstraction as a foundational research failure rather than a naming or interface defect.
- Required recurring ontological revalidation after abstractions acquire downstream methods, tests, documentation, or dependent constructions.
- Added a vocabulary-erasure test requiring the mathematics to be reconstructed without implementation-coined terminology.
- Required adversarial falsification of new abstractions and prohibited treating self-generated tests, types, documentation, or local code consistency as evidence of mathematical coherence.
- Added a semantic stop-loss requiring immediate freeze, purge, decomposition, and downstream re-audit when an abstraction has no coherent type.
- Added safeguards against semantic lock-in, self-sealing abstractions, local-consistency substitution, sunk-cost preservation, and semantic audit starvation.
- Clarified that hours of executable work inside a meaningless ontology do not establish the mathematical claims suggested by that ontology.
